Xi met Trump at the foot of the plane stairs, and the BBC calls the handshake and hug "carefully choreographed." I believe it. Nobody improvises a hug at a summit like this. Putin got a more modest greeting at a reception, which feels like a seating chart expressed in body language.

The detail I like best is Putin's timing. The Crimean bridge is lying in the water as of yesterday, and he arrives in Shenzhen to be photographed as one of three people who decide things. Either he has made peace with that contrast or he doesn't think anyone will hold it against him. The agenda hasn't been published, which means the pictures are the product.

Meanwhile Kuwait says it shot down Iranian drones aimed at Ali Al Salem, the airbase where US forces sit, and Bahrain says it repelled attacks too. The report says it was the first attack on Kuwait in more than two months. I keep coming back to that gap. For two months the war was, for some places, a thing on the news. A quiet stretch like that tends to get read as a settling, and then a US strike on Qeshm Island ends it in an evening. The lull was never a ceasefire. It was just nobody having moved yet.

Small footnote from Accra: Ghana has bought a $60m presidential jet and people are furious. It's the cleanest story today, because everyone can picture the plane and everyone knows what $60m means when prices are climbing.
